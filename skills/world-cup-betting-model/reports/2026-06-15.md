## Methodology

See `../SKILL.md` / `../model.py` for the full two-layer Dixon-Coles
approach. Layer 1 calibrates expected goals (lambda, mu) to match the
de-vigged moneyline + main total (so it is ~efficient by construction).
Layer 2 applies a research-based tilt per match (injuries, recent form),
explicitly justified below, and re-derives all markets.

Full numeric output: `2026-06-15-model-output.txt` (contains all 7 matches
modeled to date - June 14's 4 plus today's 3).
