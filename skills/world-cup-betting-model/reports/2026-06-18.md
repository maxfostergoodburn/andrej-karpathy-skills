## Caveats
- Odds are a snapshot from research time on June 18, 2026; verify live
  Fanatics prices before betting.
- Fanatics-specific odds not directly confirmed for any market — treat
  the table above as a proxy.
- Bosnia injury updates (Dzeko, Tabakovic, Kolasinac) are pre-match
  reports — confirm starting lineups before betting. If Dzeko starts,
  the Switzerland ML edge shrinks by ~1-2pp (still positive).
- Lyle Foster's prop edge is model-driven but contradicted by SA's actual
  xG output (0.07 in the opener). Use caution.
- Player prop goal shares are estimated from historical data and positional
  roles. The weakest assumption is the share itself — see sensitivity
  ranges in the props section.
- Corner kick analysis is qualitative (no formal corner model). Edge
  estimates are ranges, not point values.
- Same-game parlay prices reflect leg correlation. Cross-game legs multiply
  cleanly under independence.
- Layer 2 edges depend on the stated research tilt (judgment call). The
  Bosnia injury crisis is widely reported — if the market fully prices it
  in by kickoff, L2 edge shrinks toward L1.
