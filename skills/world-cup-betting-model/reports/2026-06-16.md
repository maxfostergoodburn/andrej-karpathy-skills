## Per-match findings

### 1. France vs Senegal (Group I)
Layer 2 tilt (lambda x1.05 / mu x0.90): Senegal are missing multiple key
attackers — Ismaila Sarr, Assane Diao (both injured) — plus defensive
depth (Sabaly, Jakobs out), while France have a fully-fit front line
(Mbappe, Dembele, Olise; Saliba returned to full training). Senegal's
injuries hit their most dangerous counter-attacking outlets hardest.
- **No positive-edge market found.** This game is efficiently priced.
  France ML (-225) is slightly expensive vs fair value (-206); Senegal
  (+550) is also expensive (model L2: 10.5%, fair +853). The draw at +320
  is essentially at fair value (+352 fair).
- If Fanatics offers France team total Over 1.5 near -130 or better,
  that's worth a look (fair -145, model 59.3% at L2 — not a quoted market
  but in the fair-value menu).

### 2. Iraq vs Norway (Group I) — the one real story today
Layer 2 tilt (lambda x0.95 / mu x1.10): Haaland makes his World Cup debut
alongside Odegaard in a full-strength Norway side that has lost just 1 of
its last 16 matches. Iraq offered little offensively in qualifying.
Multiple models project Norway at 2.5+ xG.

The market appears internally inconsistent: Over 2.5 is priced at -175
(model L1 61.0% / L2 65.5%) while Over 3.0 is priced at -110 (model L1
38.6% / L2 43.6%). For those two lines to be simultaneously correct, the
probability of exactly 3 goals would need to be ~24pp — the model (at this
expected-goals level) puts it at ~18-20pp. That gap is the edge source:

- 🔵 **Value: Under 3.0 (-110)** - model 56.4% (L2) vs implied 52.4% ->
  edge **+4.0pp, EV +7.7%**. In a push market, "Under 3.0" wins on 0-2
  goals and pushes on exactly 3 — your downside is 4+ goals only, which
  the model puts at 43.6% (vs market's implied ~47.6%). The push feature
  makes this marginally better than the straight-binary edge suggests.
- 🔵 **Value (modest): Over 2.5 (-175)** - model 65.5% (L2) vs implied
  63.6% -> edge **+1.9pp, EV +3.0%**. Decent at low edge given the
  expensive price.
- Norway ML (-460): L2 edge +0.6pp (essentially fair — don't pay the
  juice).

### 3. Argentina vs Algeria (Group J)
Layer 2 tilt (lambda x1.05 / mu x0.93): Algeria CB Ramy Bensebaini

- Fair-value note: If Fanatics' live board shows Argentina ML near -210 or

## Parlay recommendations

Today's slate is a one-game story: **Iraq vs Norway total markets** are the
only source of meaningful positive edge. France/Senegal and Argentina/Algeria
are too efficiently priced to build parlay legs from.

### Best single bet: Iraq vs Norway Under 3.0 (-110)
Model 56.4% (L2) vs implied 52.4%, edge +4.0pp, EV +7.7%. The structural
inconsistency between Over 2.5 (-175) and Over 3.0 (-110) is the alpha
here — the market has priced "exactly 3 goals as a push" at a rate that
overshoots relative to the Over 2.5 anchor.

### Cross-match combined parlay (one leg per match)
Given no real positive-edge legs in France/Senegal or Argentina/Algeria,
an honest "all 3 games" combined parlay would require accepting negative-edge
legs. Doing so in a combined parlay compounds the vig and hurts combined EV
even more. **Not recommended today** — single-leg Under 3.0 in Iraq/Norway
is the cleaner play.

## Caveats
- Odds are a snapshot from research time on June 16, 2026; verify live
  Fanatics prices before betting.
- Fanatics-specific odds not directly confirmed for any market — treat
  the table above as a proxy.
- Iraq vs Norway's Under 2.5 price (+145) used for calibration is an
  estimate — only Over 2.5 (-175) was directly confirmed. If the real
  Under 2.5 price differs materially, re-run before betting.
- The O/U 3.0 (-110/-110) integer total for Iraq vs Norway has a push at
  exactly 3 goals. The model's binary P(over_3.0) / P(under_3.0) split
  approximates (but does not perfectly model) the push-adjusted pricing
  structure. Flag the Under 3.0 edge as an approximation; the push feature
  makes the bet somewhat *better* than the raw +4.0pp edge implies (push =
  partial win), but the push-adjusted edge is not separately computed here.
- Layer 2 edges depend on the stated research tilt (judgment call) and the
  calibration anchor (Over 2.5 -175 for Iraq/Norway). The Haaland debut +
  Norway dominance read is widely shared — if the market already fully
  priced it in, L2 edge shrinks toward L1.
- Same-game parlay: combining Over 2.5 (-175) + Under 3.0 (-110) in the
  same game produces a positively correlated set (both win if the game
  lands on exactly 3 goals — push for Under, win for Over). An SGP price
  from Fanatics may not reflect this correlation cleanly.
