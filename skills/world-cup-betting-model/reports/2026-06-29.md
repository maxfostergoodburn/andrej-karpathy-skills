## Caveats
- Odds are a snapshot from research time on June 29, 2026; verify live
  Fanatics prices before betting.
- Fanatics-specific odds not directly confirmed for any market — treat
  the table above as a proxy.
- The model covers 90-minute outcomes only. "To advance" markets
  incorporate extra time and penalty probabilities that this model does
  not compute.
- Knockout matches tend to be more cautious than group games; the Layer 2
  tilts attempt to account for this, but the base calibration uses the
  90-minute moneyline which already reflects knockout dynamics.
- Germany vs Paraguay odds vary significantly across books (-245 to -340
  for Germany ML). The model uses the mid-range consensus (-278). If your
  book has Germany at -340+, the ML edge disappears — focus on BTTS No
  instead.
- Paraguay's "never scored in a WC knockout" stat spans a small sample
  (5 matches across 1998-2010). It's directionally useful but not
  statistically conclusive on its own — the model's edge comes from the
  expected-goals calibration, not the historical stat.
- Player prop goal shares are estimated from positional roles and tournament
  data. Vinicius at 40% is conservative given his 4 goals in 3 games (he's
  actually running at ~57% share), but regression to mean is assumed.
- Corner kick analysis is qualitative (no formal corner model). Edge
  estimates are ranges, not point values.
- Same-game parlay prices reflect leg correlation. Cross-game legs multiply
  cleanly under independence.
