## Methodology

See `../SKILL.md` / `../model.py` for the full two-layer Dixon-Coles
approach. Layer 1 calibrates expected goals (lambda, mu) to match the
de-vigged moneyline + main total (so it is ~efficient by construction).
Layer 2 applies a research-based tilt per match (injuries, recent form),
explicitly justified below, and re-derives all markets.

Full numeric output: `2026-06-14-model-output.txt`.

## Per-match findings

### 1. Germany vs Curacao
Layer 1 confirms the model is sane (92.8% vs 93.0% de-vigged market home
win). **No positive edge found** in 1X2 or the 4.5 total in either layer -
this game is priced efficiently given how lopsided it is. Fair-value menu
highlights: Germany -1.5 handicap fair price -568 (85.0% win by 2+),
Germany -2.5 fair -229 (69.6% win by 3+), BTTS No fair -159 (61.3%). If
Fanatics' live line on any of these beats the fair price, there's edge;
otherwise this game offers nothing actionable beyond Over 4.5 (-104,
~50/50, no edge).

### 2. Netherlands vs Japan
Layer 2 tilt (+6%/+4% to lambda/mu): both teams are missing starting
defensive pieces - Netherlands without Timber and de Ligt (their starting
CB pairing options), Japan without captain/DM Wataru Endo (ruled out and
retired days before kickoff) plus Mitoma and Minamino in attack. Net effect
leans toward more goals on both sides.
- BTTS Yes (-129): model 57.0% vs implied 56.3% -> **edge +0.7pp, EV +1.2%**
- Over 2.5 (-116): model 54.0% vs implied 53.7% -> **edge +0.3pp, EV +0.6%**

### 3. Ivory Coast vs Ecuador
Layer 2 tilt (-8%/-8% to lambda/mu): both teams have elite recent defensive
records - Ecuador conceded only 5 goals in 18 qualifiers (13 clean sheets),
Ivory Coast kept clean sheets through qualifying and is unbeaten in 5 of
last 6 (incl. a 2-1 win over France). Market's Under-2.5 lean may not go
far enough.
- BTTS No (-161): model 63.9% vs implied 61.7% -> **edge +2.2pp, EV +3.6%**
- Under 2.5 (-250): model 73.2% vs implied 71.4% -> **edge +1.8pp, EV +2.5%**
- Draw (+185): model 35.8% vs implied 35.1% -> **edge +0.7pp, EV +2.0%**

### 4. Sweden vs Tunisia
Layer 2 tilt (+10%/+8% to lambda/mu): Sweden's attack (Gyokeres hat-trick
in playoff win, Isak fit) is in form, while Tunisia lost their last
tune-up 5-0 to Belgium and fired their coach after an AFCON Round-of-16
exit - a steep recent decline despite a historically elite WC-qualifying
defensive record (0 goals conceded in 10 qualifiers).
- **Over 2.5 (+119): model 48.7% vs implied 45.7% -> edge +3.0pp, EV +6.7%**
  (best single edge across the whole slate)
- Sweden ML (-110): model 51.9% vs implied 52.4% -> edge -0.5pp (~fair)

## Caveats

  (see table above) - treat as a proxy for what Fanatics likely offers.

  tilt magnitude (a judgment call, not statistically fit).
