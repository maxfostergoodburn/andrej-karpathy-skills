## Methodology

See `../SKILL.md` / `../model.py`. Layer 1 de-vigs the moneyline to get a
fair win probability (efficient by construction - edges here are just the
vig). Layer 2 applies a research-based tilt per fight (form, injuries, style
matchups), explicitly justified below, and rescales method-of-victory and
distance/round-total markets accordingly.

Full numeric output: `2026-06-14-model-output.txt`.

## Per-fight findings

### 1. Topuria vs Gaethje (LW Title)
Layer 2 tilt (+2pp to Topuria): 17-0, on a 9-fight UFC win streak with 3
consecutive finishes of former/current champions (Volkanovski, Holloway,
Oliveira); Gaethje is 0-2 in undisputed title fights and dealing with
fight-week personal distractions.
- 🟢 **Safe: Topuria by KO/TKO (-210)** - model 69.4% vs implied 67.7% ->
  edge **+1.7pp, EV +2.5%**
- Ends inside distance (-1200): model 94.0% vs implied 92.3% -> edge +1.7pp,
  EV +1.8% - real but thin given the extreme price (risk $1200 to win $100).
- Moneylines themselves show no edge (Topuria -525 priced slightly ahead of
  even our bumped-up 82.1% model).

### 2. Pereira vs Gane (Interim HW Title)
Layer 2 tilt (-2pp to Pereira / +2pp to Gane): Pereira (38) is moving up two
weight classes to heavyweight for the first time against a younger,
technically sharper Gane who profiles well over 5 rounds. Market is already
pick'em (-110/-110), so this is a small lean.
- 🔵 **Value: Gane by Decision (+275)** - model 27.7% vs implied 26.7% ->
  edge **+1.1pp, EV +4.0%**
- Gane ML (-110): model 52.0% vs implied 52.4% -> edge -0.4pp (~fair, no
  real edge on the moneyline itself).
- No round-total/distance price was found for this fight despite searching.

### 3. O'Malley vs Zahabi
Layer 2 tilt (-2pp to O'Malley / +2pp to Zahabi): O'Malley is 2 fights
removed from major hip-labrum surgery; Zahabi's elite defense (~69% sig
strike defense) is well-suited to a length/reach-based gameplan.
- 🟢🔵 **Best combo on the card - Goes the distance (-160)**: model 66.5% vs
  implied 61.5% -> edge **+5.0pp, EV +8.1%** (high probability AND the
  biggest "normal" edge on the slate)
- 🔵 **Value: Zahabi ML (+310)** - model 25.1% vs implied 24.4% -> edge
  **+0.7pp, EV +3.0%**
- Zahabi by Decision (+500): model 18.1%, edge +1.4pp, EV +8.7% (lower
  probability, bigger payout version of the Zahabi pick).

### 4. Lewis vs Hokit
Layer 2 tilt (+7pp to Lewis): Hokit fought a brutal 15-minute war with
Curtis Blaydes just 64 days ago, is managing an ongoing hand injury that
forced a style change, and had a concerning pre-fight illness/weigh-in
incident. Lewis's only real path (a round-1 power shot) matters more than
the -430 price suggests.
- 🔵 **Biggest edge of the night - Lewis ML (+320)**: model 29.7% vs implied
  23.8% -> edge **+5.9pp, EV +24.7%**. This is the largest tilt on the card
  (+7pp) and the resulting edge is correspondingly the most speculative -
  it's entirely conditional on the turnaround/injury read being correct.
- No method-of-victory or distance odds were found for this fight.

### 5. Ruffy vs Chandler
No Layer 2 tilt: research confirms the market. Ruffy is in career-best form
(12/13 career wins by KO, latest a 2nd-round stoppage of Fiziev); Chandler
(40) is on a 3-fight skid with retirement speculation if he loses.
- **No edge found** on any quoted market (Ruffy ML -620, Ruffy KO/TKO/DQ
  -250, Ruffy Round-1 win +130 all price at ~0.0pp edge).
- Real opportunity here is **line-shopping**: Ruffy's moneyline ranged from
  -620 (FanDuel) to -750 elsewhere, and Chandler from +430 (FanDuel) to +500
  elsewhere - same model, very different prices depending on book.

### 6. Nickal vs Daukaus
No win-probability tilt (looks fairly priced), but a distance tilt
(-5pp to "Over 1.5 rounds"): Daukaus's last two UFC wins both ended in under
a minute, and Nickal's own method odds favor a finish (KO/TKO +190) over a
decision (+290) - both point toward an earlier finish than the market's
-165 "Over 1.5" price implies.
- 🔵 **Value: Under 1.5 rounds (+130)** - model 46.1% vs implied 43.5% ->
  edge **+2.6pp, EV +6.1%**
- Moneylines and method props show ~0.0pp edge (Layer 1 = Layer 2 here).

### 7. Lopes vs Garcia
Layer 2 tilt (-4.5pp to Lopes / +4.5pp to Garcia): Lopes is 1-2 in his last 3
(including a title loss), recovering from a foot fracture (wore a boot), and
carried extra fatigue as the card's backup fighter (double weigh-in). Garcia
is on a 7-fight win streak with 6 KOs in his last 7 and has never been
finished in the UFC.
- 🔵 **Value: Garcia ML (+125)** - model 47.1% vs implied 44.4% -> edge
  **+2.6pp, EV +5.9%**
- Distance tilt (-4pp to Over 1.5): Lopes himself says he's "100%" this
  won't go the distance, and Garcia's ~70% finish rate supports an earlier
  finish than the -140 "Over 1.5" price implies (model 54.3% vs implied
  58.3%, edge -4.0pp on the Over - i.e. Under is the lean, but no price was
  available for Under).

## Caveats
- Odds are a snapshot from research time on June 14, 2026 (pre-fight);
  verify live Fanatics prices before betting - the event starts 8pm ET.
- Fanatics-specific odds were not directly confirmed for any market - treat
  the table above as a proxy, and note the cross-book spreads flagged
  (Ruffy/Chandler, Nickal/Daukaus, O'Malley/Zahabi moneylines all varied
  meaningfully by book - shop around).
- Method-of-victory props are rescaled by each fighter's overall
  win-probability tilt, assuming their conditional method mix is unchanged -
  if a fighter's *style of winning* (not just chance of winning) is what's
  really different, this model won't capture that.
- Layer 2 edges are conditional on the stated research read and tilt
  magnitude (a judgment call, not statistically fit). The Lewis/Hokit +7pp
  tilt is the largest and most speculative on the card.
- A combined parlay across many legs compounds vig - legs with small
  negative Layer 2 edges (the "safe" favorites) combine into a strongly
  negative-EV parlay, which is why Option A/B above use only
  positive-edge legs.
